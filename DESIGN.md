# Internal AI interface

Enterprise workspace with Internal AI branding. Uses locally bundled Plus Jakarta Sans, pale blue-gray work surfaces, a milk-white navigation rail, indigo actions and clear selected states inspired by the attached chat UI kit. No ChatGPT or OpenAI logos or claim of affiliation.

## Shared decisions
- Primary text: #1f2a44; secondary text: #6d7d99; action indigo: #5146e5.
- Page canvas: #f7f9fc; content surfaces: white; sidebar: #fbfcfe; selected state: #eef0ff.
- Plus Jakarta Sans; titles 28–36px; body 15–16px; secondary UI 12–14px.
- Group settings in separated rows. Use segmented controls for peer views.
- Indigo visible keyboard focus, 44px mobile targets and switch hit areas.
- Respect reduced motion, reduced transparency and increased contrast preferences.
- Keep sample, disconnected and approval states explicit.

References: https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/typography and https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/color
